Common questions

Before you enquire

If yours isn’t here, just ask — Preeshani answers these personally.

Ask a question

Why don’t you list fixed prices?

Because a 120-guest single-day wedding and a 400-guest three-day celebration are not the same job, and pricing them the same would be dishonest to one of you. Send us your date and guest count and you will get a real figure, not a range.

How far in advance should we book?

For full planning, twelve to eighteen months is comfortable. For on-the-day coordination, six months is usually enough. That said, we have rescued far tighter timelines — ask before you assume it’s too late.

Do you only plan Indian weddings?

No. Indian and fusion weddings are our specialism and where most of our work sits, but we plan Western, mixed-faith and civil celebrations too — along with corporate functions and conferences.

We have already booked our vendors. Can you still help?

Yes — that is exactly what The Essentials is for. You keep the suppliers you have chosen; we take over the timeline, the briefing and the running of the day itself.

Do you travel outside Gauteng?

Yes. We are based in Alberton and work across Gauteng as standard, and we take destination weddings further afield. Travel and accommodation are quoted separately and transparently.

Will you work with our budget?

We will tell you honestly what your budget can achieve, then campaign for you on quotes to get you as close to the vision as possible. Couples consistently tell us we kept costs down without dropping the standard.
