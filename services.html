What we do

Every celebration, held end to end

Whether you want the whole thing carried for you or just a steady pair of hands on the day, there is a way in.

01 / 06

Full Wedding Planning

With a planner from Paheli, we take care of every detail — from design through to execution. Vendor selection, location scouting, menu design, décor planning, administrative tasks and negotiations, with regular communication and progress reports so you always know exactly where things stand.

  • Concept, design direction and full styling brief
  • Venue scouting, site visits and vendor sourcing
  • Quotes, negotiation, contracts and budget tracking
  • Full timeline build and on-the-day execution
02 / 06

On‑the‑Day Coordination

For couples who have already done the hard yards. We specialise in end-phase coordination for brides who have secured their vendors, quotes and contracts, and now need the day itself run properly — a customised timeline, every supplier briefed, and the whole thing managed from behind the scenes so you are a guest at your own wedding.

  • Detailed, customised wedding-day timeline
  • Vendor confirmation and full briefing pack
  • Rehearsal and final walk-through
  • Setup supervision and pack-down oversight
03 / 06

Online Planning Tools

We offer flexible online and virtual planning tools to help you create the wedding of your dreams, with as much or as little guidance as you need. Ideal if you want to drive the planning yourself but would rather not start from a blank page.

  • Planning checklists and timeline templates
  • RSVP management and guest list tracking
  • Mood boards and floorplans
  • Optional expert consultations along the way
04 / 06

Décor, Design & Styling

Event design built around one clear idea and carried consistently through every room. Mandap and ceremony design, florals, tablescapes, lighting and custom menu development — sourced, art-directed and supervised on the day.

  • Mandap, ceremony and stage design
  • Florals, tablescapes and lighting direction
  • Custom menu development with your caterer
  • Stationery and signage direction
05 / 06

Engagements & Pre‑Wedding Functions

Proposals, engagement parties, mehndi and sangeet, religious ceremonies and anniversary celebrations — planned with the same rigour as the wedding day itself, including choreographer sourcing and trend consultation.

  • Proposal planning and engagement parties
  • Mehndi, sangeet and religious ceremonies
  • Choreographer and entertainment sourcing
  • Anniversary and milestone celebrations
06 / 06

Corporate, Conference & Destination

The same planning discipline, applied to a different room. Corporate functions and conferences, gala evenings, and destination weddings further afield — where the logistics get harder and the run sheet matters more, not less.

  • Corporate functions, conferences and gala evenings
  • Destination weddings and multi-day programmes
  • Guest travel, transfers and accommodation blocks
  • Supplier coordination across provinces or borders

The process

How a Paheli wedding comes together

The same four stages, whichever package you choose.

01

Consultation

We meet, you talk, we listen. Date, venue, guest count, the traditions you want kept — and what you would quietly rather skip.

02

Design & budget

A concept, a supplier shortlist and a realistic budget. We get you multiple quotes and negotiate them before anything is signed.

03

Planning

Bookings, contracts, menus, floorplans, RSVPs and the timeline — with regular progress reports so nothing is a surprise.

04

The day

Our team is on site from setup to pack-down. Suppliers report to us, not to you. You get to actually be there.
