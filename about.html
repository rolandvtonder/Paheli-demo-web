Meet your planner

The woman behind Paheli

Eight years of weddings, a hospitality degree, and a background in international corporate events — which is exactly why nothing here is left to chance.

8+ Over eight Years planning

Founder & Head Planner

Preeshani Goven

Before Paheli, Preeshani spent years in the hotel industry and in corporate events, working with international brands — the kind of environment where a run sheet either holds or it doesn’t, and where there is no version of the evening in which you improvise. That training is the foundation the whole business sits on.

She founded Paheli Weddings & Events to bring that same discipline to a day that actually matters — and to take the weight of it off the couple. She works closely with each pair to draw out what genuinely matters to them, then builds the celebration around it: the vendors, the design, the timings, the family dynamics, and the contingency plan that nobody ever has to hear about.

Couples describe her the same way, over and over: organised, calm, honest about budget, and relentless on detail. Suppliers say the same. That consistency is the point.

“I love being able to share in the love of two individuals — and I am blessed that I get to work with amazing clients and suppliers.” Preeshani Goven, Founder

How we work

Four things we hold to

These aren’t slogans. They are the reasons the same phrases keep turning up in our reviews.

Your story, not a template

We start with what actually matters to you two — the families, the traditions you want kept, the ones you don’t — and design outward from there.

Honest about budget

We campaign for you on quotes and keep the numbers visible. Couples tell us we kept costs down without dropping the standard — that is the whole job.

There is always a plan B

Weather, late suppliers, a family surprise on the morning of. It gets handled quietly, and usually you never find out it happened.

The parents too

An Indian wedding is not just the couple’s day. We take the pressure off the families as well — and they notice.
